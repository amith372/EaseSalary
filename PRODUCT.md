# Product — EaseSalary
A Hebrew web app for a family that employs a live-in foreign caregiver and has no payroll skill.
The user marks what departed from an ordinary month and exports one salary sheet.

- Register: an operational tool, used monthly; calm and trustworthy over expressive.
- Language: Hebrew, right-to-left throughout.
- Guiding rule: choose the option that requires the user to know less.
- Scope, audience and what is excluded: `specs.md` Part 1. Behaviour: `specs.md` Part 2.
- Never propose a feature, field, flow or copy change without the user's approval (CLAUDE.md rule 4).
